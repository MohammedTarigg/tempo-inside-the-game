How it works

Each player has a ledger kept in code: his card, his running against his own usual, when he last looked. One agent for each team proposes what is at stake and why a moment happened, and a director chooses. A model only writes the sentence, and a checker rejects any number the record does not hold. The commentator's voice only decides when something shows

The whole system

Scroll sideways to follow it. The inputs on the left are the feeds a Premier League match has today. In the hackathon our generator writes the same two feeds in the same shapes. Data reaches us before the picture reaches the viewer. Fast things are decided in code within a tenth of a second. Anything written goes through the agents and is ready before the next pause. Each screen shows a cue when its own picture reaches that match time

The stake card, frame by frame

Runs 25 times a second. A small model scores each defender near the ball, and the card on a booked player has two levels. Tested on seven real matches: the faint card is on him before 86 in 100 of his challenges

flowchart TD
  F["Tracking frame arrives
about 1 to 3 s after the pitch"]:::src --> NEAR(["Is a defender within 12 m
of a live ball?"]):::ask
  NEAR -- "no" --> NO["Show nothing"]:::quiet
  NEAR -- "yes" --> FEAT["Build his last 2 seconds:
the ball, his movement, the two opponents
nearest the ball, his nearest team-mate,
who has the ball"]:::code
  FEAT --> NET["Small network, 1.4 ms for 22 players
Score: chance he makes a challenge
in the next 1.5 s"]:::model
  NET --> WHO(["Is he carrying something?
booked, or far below his usual running"]):::ask
  WHO -- "no" --> NO
  WHO -- "yes" --> LO(["Score above 0.05?"]):::ask
  LO -- "no" --> NO
  LO -- "yes" --> FAINT["Faint card on him
held until the score has been low for 2 s
about 3 s each, 73 a match"]:::code
  FAINT --> HI(["Score above 0.20?"]):::ask
  HI -- "no" --> RULE
  HI -- "yes" --> FULL["Full card on him
15 a match"]:::code
  FULL --> RULE(["Producer's rule on,
and under the cap?"]):::ask
  RULE -- "no" --> NO
  RULE -- "yes" --> STAMP["Cue stamped with this match time"]:::code
  STAMP --> OUT["Each screen shows it when
its own picture gets there"]:::screen
  GUARD["Guard: the score reads nothing
after this frame, so it cannot know
whether a challenge or a foul follows"]:::model -.-> NET

Twin search, from a goal to the replay

Starts when a goal or big chance lands and must finish before the replay airs, usually 20 to 40 seconds later. If any check fails, the plain replay is used and nothing is claimed

flowchart TD
  G["Goal or big chance"]:::src --> CUT["Cut the attack
from winning the ball to the shot"]:::code
  CUT --> NORM["Put it in a common frame
attacking left to right, mirrored if needed
positions relative to ball and goal"]:::code
  NORM --> VEC["Build the vector
ball, 6 attackers, 6 defenders
at 5 points in the move"]:::code
  VEC --> S1["Search this match
in memory"]:::code
  VEC --> S2[("Search the season
Azure AI Search")]:::store
  S1 --> CAND["Up to 20 nearest attacks
that ended differently"]:::code
  S2 --> CAND
  CAND --> NEAR(["Is the nearest one close enough?
closer than 95 in 100 random pairs"]):::ask
  NEAR -- "no" --> PLAIN["Plain 3D replay"]:::quiet
  NEAR -- "yes" --> PAIR["Pair up the players in the two attacks
by role and position"]:::code
  PAIR --> TIME["Line the two attacks up in time
find the point where they part"]:::code
  TIME --> DIFF(["How many players differ
at the parting point?"]):::ask
  DIFF -- "none, or more than two" --> PLAIN
  DIFF -- "one or two" --> REL(["Is the differing player
near the path the ball then took?"]):::ask
  REL -- "no" --> PLAIN
  REL -- "yes" --> AG["Both team agents see the twin
Director writes the scene"]:::model
  AG --> CHK(["Verifier: minutes, names
and the difference match the record"]):::ask
  CHK -- "fails twice" --> PLAIN
  CHK -- "passes" --> SCENE["Twin replay cue
positions for both attempts,
camera, who to light"]:::screen
